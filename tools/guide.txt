Robots
@items robots:farm_robot robots:breeder_robot robots:lumberjack_robot robots:organizer_robot robots:robot_terminal
Use a robot item on a block to stand it there. That block is its home, and you are its owner.

Right-click: inventory (9 stacks).
Punch: what is it doing?
Sneak + right-click: pause.
Sneak + punch: pick it up.

Robot Terminal: a screen of every robot in range, with pause buttons and each robot's settings.

Only the owner can open, change or take a robot.
---
Charging Robots
@items robots:charging_pad power_cell copper_cable
By hand: right-click a robot with a Power Cell for 1,000 J.

Automatic: wire a Robot Charging Pad to your power with Copper Cable. Below 20% a robot walks to a pad within 16 blocks of home, fills up and goes back to work.

A robot standing on a pad is always topped up. Right-click the pad to see its charge.
---
Farm Robot
@items robots:farm_robot minecraft:wheat_seeds minecraft:iron_hoe
Harvests and replants ripe crops within 8 blocks of home and takes the harvest to the nearest chest.

Also cuts pumpkins, melons, sugar cane, cactus and nether wart.

With seeds it plants empty farmland. It keeps 16 seeds of each kind and its hoe, and fetches more from a chest when it runs out.
---
Marking an Area
@items robots:field_marker minecraft:iron_hoe
Stand one Field Marker within 2 blocks of a robot's home and another at the opposite corner, up to 17 blocks away. The robot takes both and works only that rectangle.

Farm Robot: a field. With a hoe and seeds it tills the dirt and grass there and plants as it goes.
Breeder: a pen. Only animals inside count.
Lumberjack: a tree farm.

The terminal's Clear field button undoes it.
---
Animal Breeder Robot
@items robots:breeder_robot minecraft:wheat
Takes breeding food from a chest near home and feeds pairs of adult animals within 8 blocks.

It stops at 12 animals of a kind, babies included, so pens do not overflow.

Keep the right food in the chest: wheat for cows and sheep, seeds for chickens, carrots for pigs.

Switch kinds of animal on and off at a Robot Terminal.
---
Lumberjack Robot
@items robots:lumberjack_robot minecraft:iron_axe minecraft:oak_sapling
Give it an axe. It fells trees within 8 blocks of home from the top down, replants a sapling and picks up what the leaves drop.

Logs go to the nearest chest. It keeps its axe and 16 saplings, and takes a new axe from a chest when one breaks.

It only cuts trunks that grow from the ground and have natural leaves. Keep log buildings from touching its trees.
---
Organizer Robot
@items robots:organizer_robot minecraft:chest
Empties an input chest into the other chests within 8 blocks of home.

Unlabelled chests: a stack goes where that item already is, or into an empty chest.

The input chest is the one with an "input" sign, or else the unlabelled chest nearest home.

Items with nowhere to go stay in the input chest.

Chest Labels
@items minecraft:oak_sign minecraft:item_frame minecraft:chest
Put a sign on a chest and write what belongs in it, one label per line:

ores, logs, iron, oak log: items with those words in their name.
wood, metal, stone, food: wider groups.
@voltworks: everything from a mod.
misc: whatever has no other home.

An item frame on a chest names one exact item.
