Robots
@items robots:farm_robot robots:breeder_robot robots:organizer_robot robots:robot_core
Worker robots that walk around a home spot, carry items and do a job for you.

Use a robot item on a block to stand it there. That block is its home.

Each one holds 9 stacks and runs on energy: 10,000 J when full.
---
Using a Robot
@items robots:robot_terminal
Right-click: open its inventory.
Punch: ask what it is doing and what it needs.
Sneak + right-click: pause or resume.
Sneak + punch: pick it up. It keeps its energy and drops what it carried.

Robot Terminal: right-click for a screen of every robot in range. Pause them, see what each needs and change its settings.

Nothing else can hurt a robot.
---
Charging Robots
@items robots:charging_pad power_cell copper_cable
By hand: right-click a robot with a Power Cell for 1,000 J.

Automatic: wire a Robot Charging Pad to your power with Copper Cable. Below 20% a robot walks to a pad within 16 blocks of home, fills up and goes back to work.

A robot standing on a pad is always topped up. Right-click the pad to see its charge.
---
Farm Robot
@items robots:farm_robot minecraft:wheat_seeds minecraft:iron_hoe
Harvests ripe crops within 8 blocks of home, replants them and takes the harvest to the nearest chest.

Give it seeds and it plants empty farmland. It keeps 16 seeds of each kind and its hoe when it unloads.

Works with wheat, carrots, potatoes and beetroot.
---
Marking a Field
@items robots:field_marker minecraft:iron_hoe
Stand one Field Marker within 2 blocks of a Farm Robot's home and another at the opposite corner, up to 17 blocks away.

The robot takes both and works only that rectangle.

With a hoe and seeds it tills the dirt and grass there and plants as it goes. It never tills outside a marked field.

Add water yourself.
---
Animal Breeder Robot
@items robots:breeder_robot minecraft:wheat
Takes breeding food from a chest near home and feeds pairs of adult animals within 8 blocks.

It stops at 12 animals of a kind, babies included, so pens do not overflow.

Keep the right food in the chest: wheat for cows and sheep, seeds for chickens, carrots for pigs.
---
Organizer Robot
@items robots:organizer_robot minecraft:chest
Empties an input chest into the other chests within 8 blocks of home.

Unlabelled chests: a stack goes where that item already is, or into an empty chest.

The input chest is the one with an "input" sign, or else the unlabelled chest nearest home.

Items with nowhere to go stay in the input chest.
---
Chest Labels
@items minecraft:oak_sign minecraft:item_frame minecraft:chest
Put a sign on a chest and write what belongs in it, one label per line:

ores, logs, iron, oak log: items with those words in their name.
wood, metal, stone, food: wider groups.
@voltworks: everything from a mod.
misc: whatever has no other home.

An item frame on a chest names one exact item.
