Robots
@items robots:farm_robot robots:breeder_robot robots:rancher_robot robots:butcher_robot robots:lumberjack_robot robots:fisher_robot robots:guard_robot robots:organizer_robot robots:robot_terminal robots:charging_pad power_cell
Use a robot item on a block: that block is its home and you are its owner. Only the owner can open, change or take it.

Right-click: inventory. Punch: what is it doing? Sneak + right-click: pause. Sneak + punch: pick it up.

Robot Terminal: every robot in range, with pause buttons and settings.

Power: right-click with a Power Cell (1,000 J), or wire a Robot Charging Pad to your power. Below 20% a robot walks to a pad within 16 blocks of home and fills up.

Farm Robot
@items robots:farm_robot minecraft:wheat_seeds minecraft:iron_hoe
Harvests and replants ripe crops within 8 blocks of home and takes the harvest to the nearest chest.

Also cuts pumpkins, melons, sugar cane, cactus and nether wart.

With seeds it plants empty farmland. It keeps 16 seeds of each kind and its hoe, and fetches more from a chest when it runs out.
---
Marking an Area
@items robots:field_marker minecraft:iron_hoe
Stand one Field Marker within 2 blocks of a robot's home and another at the opposite corner, up to 17 blocks away. The robot takes both and works only that rectangle.

Farm Robot: a field. With a hoe and seeds it tills the dirt and grass there and plants as it goes.
Breeder, Rancher, Butcher: a pen. Only animals inside count.
Lumberjack: a tree farm.

Only markers you placed yourself count. A robot keeps its area until the terminal's Clear field button undoes it.

Animal Robots
@items robots:breeder_robot robots:rancher_robot robots:butcher_robot minecraft:wheat minecraft:shears
Breeder: feeds pairs of adult animals with food from a chest near home, up to 12 of a kind. Keep wheat, seeds or carrots in the chest.

Rancher: shears sheep and picks up the wool, eggs, feathers and leather lying around.

Butcher: when there are more than 6 of a kind, it culls adults and collects the meat. It never touches babies, pets, or named, tamed or saddled animals.

Switch kinds of animal on and off at a terminal.
---
Lumberjack Robot
@items robots:lumberjack_robot minecraft:iron_axe minecraft:oak_sapling
Give it an axe. It fells trees within 8 blocks of home from the top down, replants a sapling and picks up what the leaves drop.

Logs go to the nearest chest. It keeps its axe and 16 saplings, and takes a new axe from a chest when one breaks.

It only cuts trunks that grow from the ground with natural leaves on top. Logs touching planks, glass, doors, stairs or slabs are left alone.
---
Guard and Fisher
@items robots:guard_robot robots:fisher_robot minecraft:fishing_rod
Guard: fights hostile mobs within 12 blocks of home and takes their drops to a chest. Named or leashed mobs are left alone, and so are Endermen and Piglins unless angry. Creepers can be switched off at a terminal, as they may explode.

Fisher: give it a home near open water with a dry bank. It fishes and takes the catch to a chest (fish and junk, no treasure).
---
Organizer Robot
@items robots:organizer_robot minecraft:chest
Empties an input chest into the other chests within 8 blocks of home.

Unlabelled chests: a stack goes where that item already is, or into an empty chest.

The input chest is the one with an "input" sign, or else the unlabelled chest nearest home.

Items with nowhere to go stay in the input chest.

Chest Labels
@items minecraft:oak_sign minecraft:item_frame minecraft:chest
Put a sign on a chest and write what belongs in it, one label per line:

ores, logs, iron, oak log: items with those words in their name.
wood, metal, stone, food: wider groups.
@voltworks: everything from a mod.
misc: whatever has no other home.

An item frame on a chest names one exact item.
